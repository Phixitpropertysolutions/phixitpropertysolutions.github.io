How it works

Simple. Transparent. Efficient.

PHIX-IT keeps the full repair journey organised from the moment a problem is reported until the work is complete.

STEP 01
📱

Repair reported

A repair is submitted by the tenant or letting agent with the details needed to begin.

STEP 02
✅

Agent reviews

The letting agent reviews the request and approves or declines it through the portal.

STEP 03
👷

Contractor allocated

PHIX-IT allocates the right tradesperson based on the repair type and service area.

STEP 04
🔧

Repair completed

Progress, messages, photos and documents remain visible throughout the repair.

STEP 05
💷

Invoice managed

The final invoice and supporting information are delivered securely through the platform.
