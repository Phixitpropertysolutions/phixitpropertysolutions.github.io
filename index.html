How it works

Simple. Transparent. Efficient.

You report it. We manage the repair journey from the first request through to completion and invoicing.

STEP 01
📱

You report it

The tenant or letting agent submits the repair through the online reporting system.

STEP 02
✅

Agent reviews it

The letting agent reviews the request and approves or declines it online.

STEP 03
👷

We allocate it

PHIX-IT assigns the right tradesperson based on trade, location and availability.

STEP 04
🔧

We repair it

The contractor completes the repair while progress and supporting information remain visible.

STEP 05
💷

Job complete

The invoice and final documents are delivered securely through the PHIX-IT platform.
